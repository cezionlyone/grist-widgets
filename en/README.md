# grist-widgets - English edition

Custom widgets for [Grist](https://www.getgrist.com), free to use and modify.

| Widget | What it does | URL to paste in Grist |
|---|---|---|
| [Gristcity](gristcity/) | Draws a table as an isometric 3D city: each row becomes a building that rises through its stages. Six worlds: city, garden, garage, airport, space base, harbor. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristcity/` |
| [Gristverse](gristverse/) | Your team's virtual office in pixel art: who is at the office, working remotely or away, meetings, tasks and projects. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristverse/` |
| [Gristkanban](gristkanban/) | Kanbans for everyone, without touching your tables: eleven ready-made templates, or Kanbans suggested from the tables of your document. Drag and drop, card details, filters, activity feed. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristkanban/` |
| [Gristplan](gristplan/) | Timelines and Gantt charts on your own tables: lanes, milestones, dependencies, drag to reschedule, week numbers. Seven ready-made plans, or plans suggested from your tables. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristplan/` |
| [Gristboard](gristboard/) | Dashboards without summary tables: KPIs, bar, line, donut and radar charts, global filters. Indicators suggested from your tables, read-only on your data. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristboard/` |
| [Gristprint](gristprint/) | Printable documents from your rows without writing HTML: invoices, quotes, letters, certificates, badges. Real text PDFs, batch printing. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristprint/` |

## Using a widget

1. In your Grist document, add a **Custom** widget.
2. Choose **Custom URL** and paste the widget's URL.
3. In the right panel, under **Access**, grant the access level the widget asks for.

French editions for French public services (DSFR, the French State design system): see the [dsfr](../dsfr/) folder.
