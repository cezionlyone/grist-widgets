# grist-widgets

Custom widgets for [Grist](https://www.getgrist.com), free to use and modify. Set up entirely inside the widget: templates, suggestions from your own tables, no code to touch.

## 🌍 International edition (English) - [en/](en/)

| Widget | What it does | URL to paste in Grist |
|---|---|---|
| [Gristcity](en/gristcity/) | A table drawn as an isometric 3D city: each row is a building that rises through its stages. Six worlds. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristcity/` |
| [Gristverse](en/gristverse/) | Your team's virtual office in pixel art: presence, meetings, tasks, projects, monthly timesheet. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristverse/` |
| [Gristkanban](en/gristkanban/) | Kanbans for everyone, without touching your tables: templates or Kanbans suggested from your tables. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristkanban/` |
| [Gristplan](en/gristplan/) | Timelines and Gantt charts on your own tables: lanes, milestones, dependencies, drag to reschedule. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristplan/` |
| [Gristboard](en/gristboard/) | Dashboards without summary tables: KPIs and charts suggested from your tables, read-only on your data. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristboard/` |
| [Gristprint](en/gristprint/) | Printable documents from your rows without HTML: invoices, quotes, letters, certificates, badges. | `https://cezionlyone.github.io/grist-widgets/en/gristprint/` |

Dates, numbers and currency follow your Grist document's settings.

## Using a widget / Utiliser un widget

1. In your Grist document, add a **Custom** widget / ajoutez une vue **Personnalisée**.
2. Choose **Custom URL** / **URL personnalisée** and paste the widget's URL / collez l'adresse du widget.
3. In the right panel, under **Access**, choose **Full document access** / rubrique **Accès**, choisissez l'accès complet au document.
